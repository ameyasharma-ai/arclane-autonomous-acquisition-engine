## 7. The Direct Client Closing Playbook (From Pre-Built Prototype to UPI Settlement)

The ArcLane autonomous engine does **90% of the heavy lifting**—it scrapes high-intent local businesses, classifies real photos via Gemini Vision, compiles high-converting mobile prototypes, deploys them live on `arclanestudios.space/sites/{slug}`, and writes custom outreach copy.

A sale happens when a **business owner** agrees to pay **you (Ameya Sharma)** money via UPI to claim full ownership of that website, connect their official `.com`/`.in` domain, and attach it to their Google Business Profile.

Here is the exact operational playbook from initial prospect selection to cash in the bank:

---

### Step 1: Candidate Selection & Pre-Flight Check

Before reaching out, open your Google Sheet CRM (`Google Maps Leads`) or `data_exports/crm_leads_latest.json`. Pick **5–10 businesses** for today's batch matching these criteria:
1. **Valid Indian Mobile Number:** Verified mobile number (starts with `9`, `8`, `7`, or `6`), WhatsApp-enabled.
2. **Strong Reputation Profile:** Google rating $>4.5★$ with $>40$ reviews (shows they care about their business reputation).
3. **Live Prototype Verified:** Open `https://www.arclanestudios.space/sites/{slug}` on your mobile browser to ensure the hero image, phone number, and layout look immaculate.

---

### Step 2: The Curiosity Hook (The Opening WhatsApp Pitch)

Open WhatsApp on your phone or WhatsApp Web. Copy the personalized message from `reports/<clientId>/outreach.txt` (or the Approval Queue on your dashboard).

> **THE GOLDEN RULE:** **Never mention money, pricing, or sales pitches in Message 1.**  
> The sole objective of Message 1 is to get the owner to **tap the link on their mobile screen**.

#### Verbatim WhatsApp Template (Hinglish):
```text
Namaste [Owner Name / Team Gym Paradox],

I noticed your gym has strong 4.9★ reviews in Patliputra Colony, but lacked a dedicated mobile page for direct membership inquiries.

Maine aapke gym ke liye ek custom live mobile website concept design kiya hai — jisme direct WhatsApp trial booking, workout programs, aur batch timings integrate kiye hain:

🔗 https://www.arclanestudios.space/sites/gymparadox

Aapko overall look aur concept kaisa laga? Would love your honest feedback!

— Ameya Sharma, Arclane Studios
```

---

### Step 3: Navigating the 3 Core Prospect Reactions

When owners open the link and see their **own business name, authentic facility photos, and working WhatsApp booking buttons**, they typically respond in one of three ways:

#### Reaction A: *"Kitna kharcha aayega?" / "How much does it cost?"* (High Buying Intent)
They have already bought into the visual value; they are now asking for the transaction terms.
> **Your Verbatim Response:**  
> *"Sir, normally agencies custom coding aur design ka ₹20,000–₹25,000 charge karti hain. Lekin kyunki hamara base design aapke liye already ready hai, humne Patna ke selected premium centers ke liye flat **₹4,999** ka complete package rakha hai — jisme aapka custom `.com` ya `.in` domain, pure 1 saal ki high-speed hosting, aur direct WhatsApp leads setup sab included hai.*  
> *Aap batayein, isme kuch specific change karna hai ya ye design perfect hai?"*

#### Reaction B: *"Achha laga / Bahut badhiya bana hai."* (Interest, Needs Direct Call to Action)
They like what they see but haven't taken the commercial step.
> **Your Verbatim Response:**  
> *"Thank you sir! Isko hum aapke Google Business Profile se link kar sakte hain taaki Google Maps par search karne wale clients direct WhatsApp par trial book karein. Agar aap chahein to kal subah tak aapke khud ke domain (e.g. `gymparadox.com`) par isko live launch kar dete hain. Quick 2-minute call par baat karein?"*

#### Reaction C: No Reply After 4–6 Hours
The message was read or missed during busy business hours. Queue them for the **06:00 PM Manual Calling Window**.

---

### Step 4: The 2-Minute Phone Call Script (06:00 PM Window)

If a prospect hasn't replied to WhatsApp by evening, call their direct phone number:

> **You:** *"Hello, kya meri baat [Business Name] ke owner se ho rahi hai?"*  
> **Owner:** *"Haan, boliye kaun?"*  
> **You:** *"Namaste sir, main Ameya Sharma baat kar raha hoon Arclane Studios se. Maine afternoon mein aapke gym ke liye ek live website demo banakar WhatsApp par share kiya tha. Bas 1 minute check karna tha ki kya aapko link receive hua tha?"*  
> **Owner:** *"Haan dekha tha / Nahi abhi nahi dekha."*  
> **You:** *"Sir aap abhi WhatsApp par link open karke dekhiye — humne aapke Patliputra Colony location aur photos ke sath ek 1-tap WhatsApp trial booking page banaya hai. Koi generic template nahi hai, 100% custom mobile page hai.*  
> *Aapko concept kaisa laga sir? Agar aapko pasand aaye to hum aapka direct phone map karke 24 ghante mein aapka official domain connect kar denge."*

---

### Step 5: Objection Annihilation Framework

#### Objection 1: *"Bhai website ki zaroorat nahi hai, Google Maps se customer aa jaate hain."*
> **Answer:** *"Bilkul sahi bol rahe hain sir! Yeh website Google Maps ko hatane ke liye nahi hai—yeh Google Maps ke sath lagne ke liye hai. Jo customer Google par aapko search karta hai, wo aadhi baar competing gyms ke paas chala jata hai kyunki batch timings ya fee clear nahi hoti. Is page se wo direct aapke WhatsApp par aayega."*

#### Objection 2: *"Pehle use karke dekhun to?"*
> **Answer:** *"Link to aapke haath mein hai sir! Aap apne phone par poora test kijiye. Isme hum aapke exact membership plans aur timing update karke denge."*

#### Objection 3: *"Thoda discount karo."*
> **Answer:** *"Sir, ₹4,999 mein domain kharidna, server hosting, SSL certificate aur poore saal ka maintenance sab included hai. Agar aap aaj confirm karte hain, to main ₹4,499 final karke kal morning tak aapke naam par live launch kar dunga."*

---

### Step 6: Closing the Deal & Collecting Advance Payment (UPI)

Never ask for full payment upfront—it introduces unnecessary hesitation. Ask for an **advance token**:

> *"Sir, aap ₹2,000 token advance UPI kar dijiye. Main abhi aapka custom domain register karke aapka personal WhatsApp number aur pricing configure kar deta hoon.*  
> *Kal subah jab poori site aapke domain par live ho jayegi aur aap test kar lenge, tab aap remaining ₹2,499 clear kar dena."*

Send your UPI ID / Google Pay / PhonePe QR directly into the WhatsApp chat.

---

### Step 7: 15-Minute Delivery & Retention Upsell

Once the ₹2,000 token is received:
1. **Register Custom Domain:** Purchase their domain on Hostinger, Namecheap, or Google Domains (costs ~₹600–₹800 for `.in` or `.com`).
2. **Link to Vercel:** In Vercel, attach the custom domain to the project and route it to the client's slug.
3. **Google Business Profile Linking:** Instruct (or guide) the owner to update their Google Maps listing by pasting the new custom domain into the `Website` field.
4. **Final Balance Settlement:** Send the live link under their own domain name (`https://www.{business}.com`) and request the remaining ₹2,499 balance.
5. **Seed the Retainer:** Once satisfied, introduce the monthly growth package:  
   *"Sir, har mahine naye seasonal offers, festival banners aur photos update karne ke liye humara ₹799/month ka maintenance plan hai, jisse aapko kabhi technical tension nahi leni padegi."*
