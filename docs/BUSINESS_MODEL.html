The "Value-First" Acquisition & BI Framework

A data-driven autonomous B2B acquisition framework that extracts under-monetized SMBs, manufactures custom digital assets on autopilot, and delivers instant proof of value before requesting commitments.

1. Strategic Funnel & Lifecycle Stages

From raw Google Maps coordinates to paying recurring retainers.

Weekly Target: 175 Ingested → 10 Closed (₹80k)
Stage 1 • Discovery
🎯
Hyper-Local Ingestion

Scraper initiates queries for specific metro clusters (Pune, Gurugram, Ahmedabad, Surat, Jaipur). Targets established SMBs with $\ge 40$ reviews and $\ge 4.5\star$ ratings lacking functional websites.

Engine: Playwright + Multi-Source Regex (+91 DOM hunt)
Stage 2 • Audit
🧠
14-Point AI Intelligence Audit

Gemini Flash Lite analyzes review velocity, competitor positioning, and mobile capture leakages. Generates an executive SWOT summary and customized curiosity pitch angles.

Output: reports/<id>/audit.md + outreach.json
Stage 3 • Asset Creation
💻
Autonomous Web Production

System converts the AI blueprint into a responsive Tailwind CSS landing page, initializes a Git repository, and deploys it live to Vercel in under 45 seconds per business.

Infrastructure: GitHub CLI + Vercel Production API
Stage 4 • Outreach
📲
Curiosity-Hook Dispatch

WhatsApp Web dispatches personalized messages (12:00 PM & 04:30 PM waves). Messages contain direct live demo URLs without high-pressure sales pitches.

Guardrails: 30–75s randomized delay, anti-ban spacing
Stage 5 • Inbound
🤯
Value Realization & Inbound Reply

Prospect opens their custom URL, experiencing their business name, branding, and booking buttons already functional. Typical reaction: "How much does this cost?"

Benchmark: 5% - 8% warm engagement rate
Stage 6 • Monetization
💰
Deal Closing & Expansion

Closed via structured 2-minute phone script at flat ₹7,999 launch price. Up-sell path: custom .com domain, Google Business profile optimization, and monthly maintenance retainers.

Unit Economics: 100% margin, immediate UPI settlement
