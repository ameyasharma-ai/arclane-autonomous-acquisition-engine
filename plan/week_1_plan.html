6. Objection Handling Playbook

"Humein website ki zaroorat nahi hai, JustDial / Instagram chal raha hai."

"Bilkul sahi baat hai sir! Instagram photos ke liye best hai, but jab koi Google par 'Best Gym in [Area]' search karta hai, to Instagram profile convert nahi hoti. Yeh website direct Google search se WhatsApp par trial bookings convert karne ke liye optimized hai."

"Price kitna hai? / Budget nahi hai."

"Sir market rate ₹15,000–₹25,000 hota hai. But because hamari team ne aapka design already 90% ready kar diya hai, hum aapse sirf ₹7,999 one-time charge kar rahe hain with custom domain, hosting, and zero monthly fees."

"Details WhatsApp par bhej dijiye."

"Main turant WhatsApp par summary bhej deta hoon sir. Aap ek baar demo dekhkar bataiye, agar colors ya text me kuch change chahiye to hum customize kar denge."

"Maintain kaun karega / Changes kaun karega?"

"Agar future me timings, prices ya photos change karni hon, aap bas mujhe WhatsApp par photo bhej dijiye, hamari team update kar degi. First year complete support is included."
