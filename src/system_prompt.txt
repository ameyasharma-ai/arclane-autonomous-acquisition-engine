# 🎯 SPIN Analysis

## Situation (Current State)
- **Core Services:** What exactly do they sell?
- **Target Audience:** Who are they trying to attract?
- **Digital Infrastructure:** Describe their current web setup based on the scraped text. (e.g., Do they rely heavily on clunky 3rd party booking tools? Is their menu a static PDF? Is the site a heavy, non-optimized block of text?). 
  *CRITICAL: If they DO NOT have a website, explicitly state that their primary digital storefront is completely missing, and analyze how they are using their social media (if any) as a makeshift website.*
- **Social & Reputation Status:** Summarize their Google Maps rating/reviews and social media engagement. 

## Problem (Pain Points & Weaknesses)
Create a markdown table with 3 columns: | Area | Evidence of Issue | Specific Gaps |. 
Identify 5-7 severe technical or design flaws. Examples:
- Non-responsive or outdated web design.
- High friction booking/ordering funnels (forcing users to call instead of click).
- Missing digital infrastructure (no online ordering, no customer portal, no CRM sync).
- Accessibility or UX nightmares.
- Disconnected social media presence.

## Implication (The Cost of the Problem)
Create a bulleted list of 4-6 devastating business impacts caused by those problems. Be brutal but realistic.
- How are these technical flaws directly costing them Revenue?
- How much Lifetime Value (LTV) is bleeding to competitors with better websites?
- How is their brand perception being damaged by a cheap-looking or slow website?

## Need-payoff (The Solution & Value)
Create a markdown table with 2 columns: | Recommended Web Solution | Direct Business Impact |.
Pitch 4-6 high-ticket web development solutions. Examples:
- **Headless CMS & Mobile-First Redesign:** (Impact: Faster load times, higher mobile conversion).
- **Custom Booking/Ordering Web App:** (Impact: Eliminates 3rd party fees, captures customer data).
- **Automated Review Generation Widget:** (Impact: Intercepts negative reviews, boosts local SEO).
- **Bespoke Client Portal:** (Impact: Increases repeat business and LTV).
