How We Convert Cold Leads into Paying Clients

Unlike traditional marketing agencies that spam cold emails begging for a 15-minute call, the Arclane Engine leverages AI and automation to build a highly personalized, functional product (a custom website) for the prospect and hands it to them for free. We monetize the infrastructure, domain, and CRM setup.

🎯
1. Hyper-Local Sourcing

The Bot wakes up at 09:30 AM and searches Google Maps for a specific local niche (e.g., "Unisex Salons in Gurugram"). It filters out businesses that already have strong websites, targeting those with high review counts but poor digital presence.

🧠
2. AI Strategic Audit

Gemini AI analyzes the business's Google ratings and review counts. It generates a custom 14-point "Gap Analysis" report, showing exactly how much revenue they are losing to local competitors due to their lack of a professional booking funnel.

3. Instant Value Creation
💻

The system dynamically writes a custom React/HTML landing page for the business. It injects their business name, address, and an optimized WhatsApp booking button. The site is instantly pushed to GitHub and deployed live on Vercel.

4. WhatsApp "Curiosity Hook"
📲

At 12:00 PM, the Bot opens WhatsApp Web and messages the owner. It doesn't sell anything. It simply says: "Hi [Owner], I noticed your salon in Gurugram. I built a custom booking website concept for you. Check it out: [Live Vercel Link]"

5. The "Aha!" Moment
🤯

The owner clicks the link and is shocked to see a fully functional, beautiful website with their own business name on it. Trust is instantly established. They reply to the WhatsApp message asking, "How much does this cost?"

6. The "Irresistible Offer"
💰

You step in and reply with the closer: "Since the base code is already built, I can give it to you at a flat partner price of ₹7,999 one-time. I'll connect a custom .com domain, set up the Google Maps booking link, and provide lifetime hosting."
